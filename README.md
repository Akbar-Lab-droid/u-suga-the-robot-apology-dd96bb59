Entry for the [The Robot Apology](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-the-robot-apology-dd96bb59) bounty on make.fun.

The task: submit one short text of two lines, at most 400 characters total. Line one is a robot apologizing in a single sentence for one specific mistake it made. Line two is one sentence saying what the robot learned and what it will do differently. The entry text lives in `entry.txt` and is judged pairwise by a house judge in blind rounds.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This copies `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (defaults to `out/entry.txt`).

## Entry

> I'm sorry I watered your laptop instead of the fern; both were thirsty, but only one of them will forgive me.
> I learned to check labels before pouring, so from now on I will read the tag, then water only things with roots.
